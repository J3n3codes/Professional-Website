ABOUT

About me

I completed a degree in Biomedical Engineering at the University of Mumbai and a thesis-based graduate degree in Biostatistics at New York University (NYU). I was offered admission to a Public Health PhD program at Oregon State University; this is an admission offer, not a completed degree.

Research and professional interests

My interests include quantitative research, public health, statistics, data science, responsible technology, and evidence retrieval. I am particularly interested in how research questions and evidence can better represent different geographic and resource contexts.

Selected experience

  • Research assistance and biostatistics consulting.
  • Data management, quality checks, and literature synthesis.
  • Teaching and explaining applied data analysis concepts.
  • Independent technical projects involving public data, retrieval, semantic matching, and visualization.

Writing and advocacy

Creative writing is developed under a pen name. Three manuscripts have been developed, with one in editing for a future planned publication. Activities supporting statistical literacy and accessible evidence may be added as details are finalized.
